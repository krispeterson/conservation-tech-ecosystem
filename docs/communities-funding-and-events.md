# Communities, Funding, and Events

## Communities and forums

### Broad conservation-tech communities

- **WILDLABS**  
  Global conservation technology community with discussions, groups, learning resources, events, and discovery tools.  
  <https://wildlabs.net/>

- **WILDLABS Open Source Solutions group**  
  A useful place to find discussion of low-cost hardware, open-source software, and practical field tools.  
  <https://wildlabs.net/groups/open-source-solutions>

- **WILDLABS Marine Conservation group**  
  Marine-focused discussions spanning bioacoustics, biologging, drones, remote sensing, and machine learning.  
  <https://wildlabs.net/groups/marine-conservation>

- **WildHub**  
  Peer-led community for conservation professionals. Not narrowly technical, but very relevant for implementation, peer support, and field realities.  
  <https://wildhub.community/>

- **GOSH**  
  Open science hardware community that matters for conservation sensors, field devices, repairability, and documentation culture.  
  <https://openhardware.science/>

### Product and platform communities

- **SMART Community Forum**  
  User discussion and support forum for SMART.  
  <https://forum.smartconservationtools.org/>

- **Wildlife Insights discussion group**  
  User support and announcements.  
  <https://groups.google.com/g/wildlifeinsights>

- **iNaturalist Community Forum**  
  Strong discussion forum around biodiversity observation, data quality, taxonomy, and platform use.  
  <https://forum.inaturalist.org/>

## Collaborations and alliances

- **Wildlife Insights partnership**  
  Multi-organization collaboration with major conservation and research partners plus Google as technology partner.  
  <https://www.wildlifeinsights.org/about>

- **Tech4Nature**  
  Partnership between IUCN and Huawei TECH4ALL focused on scaling digital conservation efforts.  
  <https://iucngreenlist.org/projects/tech4nature/>

- **SERCA (SMART–EarthRanger Conservation Alliance)**  
  Important alliance linking two major operational conservation platforms.  
  Start here:  
  <https://smartconservationtools.org/en-us/>  
  <https://www.earthranger.com/>

- **Conservation X Labs**  
  Innovation organization that also functions as a bridge between conservation problems, entrepreneurs, and challenge/prize mechanisms.  
  <https://conservationxlabs.com/>

## Awards, challenges, grants, and prize programs

- **WILDLABS Awards**  
  Grant program supporting early and more advanced conservation-tech efforts.  
  <https://wildlabs.net/funding-opportunity/wildlabs-awards-2026-express-your-interest-now>

- **#Tech4Wildlife Challenge**  
  WILDLABS challenge and visibility mechanism for conservation-tech projects.  
  <https://wildlabs.net/event/2026-tech4wildlife-challenge>

- **Conservation Technology Award**  
  EarthRanger / Ai2-backed support for organizations deploying or developing conservation technology.  
  <https://www.earthranger.com/conservation-tech-award>

- **XPRIZE Rainforest**  
  Major challenge program focused on autonomous biodiversity monitoring and rainforest data collection.  
  <https://www.xprize.org/competitions/rainforest>

- **Con X Tech Prize**  
  Prize ecosystem run by Conservation X Labs.  
  <https://conservationx.com/prize>

## Conferences and sector events

- **International Conservation Technology Conference (ICTC)**  
  WILDLABS-backed conference focused on major challenges and breakthroughs in conservation technology.  
  <https://wildlabs.net/event/international-conservation-technology-conference>

## Why this matters

A lot of the real leverage in conservation technology does not come from a single app.

It comes from the combination of:

- communities that help people find one another
- prizes that create early traction
- implementation alliances that reduce fragmentation
- shared forums where people solve practical deployment problems

That is why these sections belong in the repo instead of being treated as side notes.
